Call for Papers

The 1st International Conference on AI, Computing, Resilient and Autonomous Future Technologies (AICRAFT-2027) invites original research contributions addressing the latest advancements in artificial intelligence, machine learning, intelligent computing, generative AI, computer vision, autonomous systems, cybersecurity, resilient computing, IoT, edge technologies, and emerging intelligent applications. The conference provides an international platform for researchers, academicians, industry professionals, practitioners, and scholars to exchange knowledge, present innovative research, discuss emerging challenges, and explore transformative solutions in intelligent, secure, resilient, and autonomous technologies.

Authors are invited to submit full papers presenting original, previously unpublished research that is not currently under review or consideration by any other conference or journal. Submitted papers should be prepared using the IEEE double-column format and should not exceed six pages, including figures, tables, results, and references. All accepted and presented papers will be considered for submission to the IEEE Conference Publication Program for possible inclusion in the IEEE Xplore Digital Library (subject to IEEE Delhi Section approval, applicable quality requirements, and publication policies).

Conference Scope

AICRAFT-2027 invites original, high-quality research contributions from researchers, academicians, scientists, industry professionals, developers, and practitioners worldwide. The conference aims to explore recent advancements, innovative methodologies, emerging technologies, and real-world applications in artificial intelligence, advanced computing, resilient systems, autonomous technologies, and intelligent applications.

Topics of interest include, but are not limited to:

  • Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and Intelligent Computing
  • Generative AI, Large Language Models, and Foundation Models
  • Deep Learning, Reinforcement Learning, and Federated Learning
  • Explainable, Trustworthy, and Responsible AI
  • AI Agents, Agentic AI, and Intelligent Decision Support
  • Data Science, Big Data Analytics, and Intelligent Data Processing
  • Cloud, Edge, Fog, and Distributed Computing
  • Quantum, Neuromorphic, and High-Performance Computing
  • Computer Vision, Image Processing, and Multimodal Intelligence
  • Medical Imaging, Remote Sensing, and Intelligent Perception
  • Object Detection, Video Analytics, and Pattern Recognition
  • Robotics, Autonomous Systems, and Intelligent Agents
  • Autonomous Vehicles, Human-Robot Interaction, and Swarm Intelligence
  • Digital Twins, Cyber-Physical Systems, and Autonomous Decision-Making
  • Cybersecurity, AI Security, and Privacy-Preserving Computing
  • Adversarial Machine Learning, Blockchain, and Secure Computing
  • Resilient Computing, Fault-Tolerant Systems, and Zero-Trust Architecture
  • AI Governance, Ethics, Safety, and Trustworthy Intelligent Systems
  • Internet of Things (IoT), Industrial IoT, and Intelligent Connected Systems
  • Edge AI, Fog Computing, and Networked Autonomous Systems
  • Intelligent Networks, 5G/6G Technologies, and Smart Communication Systems
  • Wireless Sensor Networks, Vehicular Networks, and Network Intelligence
  • AI Applications in Healthcare, Agriculture, and Smart Cities
  • Intelligent Transportation, Smart Manufacturing, and Industry 4.0/5.0
  • Energy, Climate Intelligence, Sustainability, and Assistive Technologies

Within these thematic areas, authors are encouraged to submit research covering generative and trustworthy AI, intelligent computing, computer vision, autonomous systems, resilient cybersecurity, IoT and edge technologies, and AI-driven applications in healthcare, industrial environments, smart cities, and sustainable infrastructure. The conference welcomes theoretical contributions, experimental studies, innovative methodologies, and practical implementations addressing contemporary challenges in intelligent, secure, resilient, and autonomous technologies.
